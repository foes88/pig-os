# Wiselake to Launch PigOS in July, an AI Swine Management Platform Built on 27 Years of Expertise — Re-engineered for Global KPI Standards

### Korea's proven pig-farm software know-how, redesigned for international KPIs — targeting underserved mid-to-large farms across the US, China, Southeast Asia, and Latin America. Free to start.

---

**Seoul, South Korea — [Month] [Day], 2026** — **Wiselake**, a swine data and software company, announced that it **will launch PigOS on July 1, 2026** — a cloud-based **AI swine farm management platform** that brings the company's **27 years of pig-farm system development and operation** to farms worldwide, **re-engineered to meet the KPI standards and operating realities of overseas markets**.

PigOS adapts the domain expertise of **PigPlan**, Wiselake's swine management software operated in Korea for 27 years, into a global SaaS platform for **mid-to-large pig farms (500–5,000 sows)**. It is **free to start**.

As **African Swine Fever (ASF)** and rising feed and production costs raise the need for **data-driven precision management** across the global swine industry, PigOS standardizes breeding and productivity data to country standards so farms can **catch performance drops and risks earlier**.

## Three things that make PigOS different

**1. Built for global KPI benchmarking.**
PigOS manages the full breeding cycle (mating, farrowing, weaning) and tracks global-standard productivity KPIs — **PSY, NPD, FCR, and farrowing rate** — on a real-time dashboard, letting farms **benchmark their performance against regional and global averages**. It supports **six languages** (English, Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Brazilian Portuguese, LATAM Spanish) with country-specific units, currency, and date formats.

**2. AI analytics for every farm — not just the enterprise.**
While existing swine software reserves AI for large corporate operations, PigOS offers **AI-powered analytics to small and mid-size farms from the free tier**. The top 40 companies own just **26% of the world's sows**; the remaining **74% — mid-size farms scaling from 500 to 5,000 sows — are the most underserved segment** in farm-management software. No competitor today satisfies SME-optimization, free entry, open API, and AI simultaneously. PigOS uses **explainable AI**: its rule engine analyzes farm events and shows **why a signal fired** — the detection rule, current value, threshold, and recommended actions — so farmers can trust the result and act immediately.

**3. Four markets, one platform.**
PigOS targets the **US, China, Southeast Asia (Vietnam, Thailand), and Latin America**, with multi-farm management, six-level access control, and monthly KPI/breeding/mortality/shipment reporting. Its **multi-tenant architecture** isolates each farm's data and scales to thousands of farms.

## Business approach

PigOS launches **fully free** to focus on adoption and market validation, with **sow-count-based pricing tiers in development**.

> "The 27 years of field data and operating experience we built with PigPlan is the foundation of PigOS," said **Jin Gyo Moon, CEO of Wiselake**. "Our goal is to take swine expertise proven in Korea, align it to global KPI standards, and put it — for free — in the hands of the mid-size farms worldwide that have never had access to good software."

## How to get started

From **July 1, 2026**, farms can sign up at **pigos.io** and **start free**, on web and mobile.
