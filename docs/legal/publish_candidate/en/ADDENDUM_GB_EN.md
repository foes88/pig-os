## Article 2 (Cross-border Transfer)

1. Storage of UK farm data on Korean servers is treated as a cross-border transfer under UK GDPR, and the basis for the transfer is the UK→Korea adequacy regulations (The Data Protection (Adequacy) (Republic of Korea) Regulations 2022). An IDTA is not required. The fact of the transfer and the adequacy basis shall be stated in the Privacy Notice.
2. **Response to re-examination**: If the above adequacy regulations are amended or withdrawn, the Company shall without delay apply an alternative transfer mechanism such as an IDTA, and the rights of users shall not be affected.
3. **Onward transfer**: The onward transfer from Korea to a third country to the external AI vendor under Purpose ⑥ maintains continuity of protection through (i) a processor agreement + no-training and retention-limitation clauses, (ii) region/zero-retention options where available, and (iii) disclosure of the onward-transfer chain in the Privacy Notice. For vendors located in the United States, DPF (including the UK Extension) certification status shall be verified, but confirmation of DPF certification alone shall not be deemed to satisfy the lawfulness of the onward transfer. The role of each party (controller/processor) and the adequacy alignment of the contractual chain shall be reviewed separately.

## Article 3 (Extension of Data Subject Rights)

1. In addition to the rights provisions of the Master Terms, the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and objection under UK GDPR are guaranteed.
2. **Right to object regarding Purpose ②**: Users may object at any time to processing for anonymized/aggregated statistics purposes based on legitimate interest, and upon receipt of an objection, they are immediately excluded from future aggregation batches.

## Article 4 (Legal Basis for Purpose ② Anonymized/Aggregated Statistics)

1. Purpose ② aims to be operated in the UK not as a consent toggle but under a UK GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interest + right to object (opt-out) structure.
2. The above structure is conditioned upon completion of a Legitimate Interests Assessment (LIA, three-part test) and assurance of the effectiveness of notice and the right to object. Until the LIA is completed, it shall not be deemed that "the legitimate interest has been established," and the basis for Purpose ② is conditioned upon completion of the LIA. It reflects that the anonymization processing act itself may also require an Art. 6 basis.
3. Purposes ③④⑤ maintain opt-in (default OFF) in accordance with the global policy.

## Article 5 (Consent for Cookies · Similar Technologies)

1. Except for cookies·similar technologies strictly necessary for the provision of the Service, **non-essential cookies (analytics, performance, marketing, etc.) shall be set·read only after obtaining the user's prior consent (opt-in)** (PECR reg. 6).
2. Consent is obtained through a clear affirmative action, without using pre-ticked boxes·bundled consent, and withdrawal of consent is provided as easily as giving consent. The cookie policy discloses the purposes·retention periods·third-party recipients.

## Article 6 (Marketing · Email)

1. Electronic direct marketing follows PECR. Corporate subscriber email does not require PECR consent but requires display of the sender's identity + provision of opt-out, and UK GDPR (LIA·notice) applies concurrently.
2. Note that sole traders·partnerships are individual subscribers and thus subject to opt-in.
3. Common: notice after collection + operation of a suppression list.

## Article 7 (Deadline for Erasure · Notification)

1. Requests to exercise rights are processed within one month of receipt (extendable by two months depending on complexity·with notice of extension).
2. Upon withdrawal, individual-level (original·pseudonymized) data is erased·anonymized within a short period and is not retained long-term after withdrawal. Statistical outputs for which irreversible anonymization has been completed are not personal data and therefore persist and are not subject to recall·erasure — "no retroactive recall of already-provided portions" applies only to anonymized/aggregated outputs.

## Article 8 (Governing Law · Disputes)

1. Notwithstanding the governing law·jurisdiction provisions of the Master Terms, the protection and jurisdiction conferred by the mandatory rules of the UK (including personal data protection·consumer protection) are not excluded (savings clause).
2. The Company prepares for the possibility of a determination that individual-business-operator farm owners hold a consumer-like status.
